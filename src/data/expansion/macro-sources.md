# Macro content source notes

This file documents the source registry used for 100 articles and 10 stubs in macro.json. The articles are original Hebrew explanations with original illustrative calculations. Sources are attached per entry for further reading, with models distinguished from unconditional empirical claims. No current interest rates, tax rates, country membership totals, forecasts or investment recommendations are asserted.

Primary sources include central banks (RBA, Federal Reserve, ECB, Bank of England, Bank of Canada, HKMA), IMF, World Bank, WTO, OECD, BIS, US BEA, US International Trade Administration, IFSWF, MIT OpenCourseWare and the authors/publishers of economic research. Pages were verified through browsing during authoring. Some NBER and HKMA requests intermittently returned access errors; research titles were independently located in search, and the institutional landing page is retained for HKMA. These are source-access limitations, not claims that inaccessible text was read.

The complete registry is below. The dated source title, when present, refers to that publication and is not a claim that its numerical policy settings remain current. Examples throughout are hypothetical.
