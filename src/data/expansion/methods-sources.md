# Methods and applied economics content: source and structure audit

The final `methods.json` contains exactly 100 full Hebrew articles and 10 explicitly marked stubs: 78 research entries and 32 labor/environment entries. Each full article has three topic-specific sections, a worked example, two FAQ answers, two advantages, two limitations, and primary or official technical sources. Full article bodies contain 177–209 whitespace-delimited words. Stub bodies contain 76–84 words and identify specific questions for future expansion.

Unique slugs and titles, required fields, internal related links, article/stub counts, category assignments, and body length were checked. Worked examples were reviewed for arithmetic and for the assumptions needed to interpret the result. Numerical examples are illustrative rather than claims about current economic conditions.

## Source availability

A read-only URL audit of all 73 distinct cited source URLs returned HTTP 200 for 68 URLs and HTTP 403 for five URLs. No cited URL returned HTTP 404. Most sources are statistical agencies, public institutions, research organizations, peer research, or official technical documentation. HTTP success verifies access, not independent approval of every interpretation in an article. This is editorial educational content and has not received an independent subject-matter expert review.
