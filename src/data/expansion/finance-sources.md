# Finance expansion — authoring and validation record

Delivered: 100 full Hebrew articles and 10 explicitly marked stubs in `finance.json`.

The full articles contain 178–205 whitespace-delimited words in the main body and 284–327 across the main educational fields. Each has three subject-specific sections, an illustrative example, two FAQs, and at least two usefulness and limitation points. The stubs contain 81–89 body words and specific questions for expansion.

Validation checked exact counts, unique titles and slugs, word counts, headings, required arrays, and every related slug against the combined repository catalog. No broken related links were found. Examples were manually checked during authoring; figures are educational assumptions, not current market observations.

Sources were opened through web browsing during authoring. Primary publishers include SEC, FINRA, BIS, IFRS Foundation, CFA Institute, Options Industry Council, CME, ICMA, ESMA, Cboe, S&P Dow Jones Indices, HMRC and Bank of England. The covered-bond source was replaced with the accessible BIS paper; additional topic-specific sources were added for puttable and convertible bonds, private placements, volatility smile and PIK debt.

Scope and limits: introductory sources support general mechanisms; some articles also explain standard analytical implications and independently constructed examples. Jurisdiction-specific sources illustrate their own frameworks and are not presented as Israeli legal or tax rules. These are educational entries, not personalized investment advice or security recommendations. Dates in the schema follow the commissioned content contract. Some CFA sources expose an overview while the full curriculum requires membership. No external professional editorial certification is claimed.
